Collection notes

A garden, stitched into festive wear

“We wanted Diwali to feel like a picnic in an English garden. Gingham laid on grass, flowers tucked in hair.”

For Festive 2026 we reimagined our atelier favourites, peplums, shararas and palazzos, in a soft garden palette. Checks in lavender, peony pink and ecru become the canvas, printed over with oversized monstera, hibiscus and daffodil.

Gingham baseLavender, blush and mustard checks
Tropical botanicalMonstera, hibiscus, daffodil
Tiered shararaTwo and three tier flare, made to twirl
Gota + mirrorFine kinari, triangle gota, shisha
