Our story

Born from Blush. Inspired by Aryaana. Made for little moments.

How it began

For 15 years, Blush has been creating Indian and formal wear for women at BLUSH Kanak Chandhok, Shahpur Jat.

Over the years, our clients often asked, “When are you going to make something for our little ones?”

Then came Aryaana — our little muse, who wore her mother’s creations for festivals and celebrations and received endless compliments.

Soon, friends and clients began asking for outfits for their little girls, and for beautiful mother-daughter twinning moments.

And that’s how Blush Tiny Blossoms came to life — a little world of joyful, elegant and beautifully crafted outfits, made for the little girls who make every celebration brighter.

For twirls, togetherness and memories that last a lifetime.

What we care about

Three things, every piece

Soft first

Fabrics chosen to sit gently on young skin, through a long evening of celebrations.

Made to move

A relaxed fit, side-seam pockets in the shararas and tiers that are cut to twirl. Nothing stiff, nothing scratchy.

Handwork that shows

Gota, sequin and resham handwork, placed where it catches the light and kept away from where it would bother.

Festive ’26 · collection notes

The English Garden Edit

“We wanted Diwali to feel like a picnic in an English garden. Gingham laid on grass, flowers tucked in hair.”

For Festive 2026 we reimagined our atelier favourites, peplums, shararas and palazzos, in a soft garden palette. Checks in lavender, peony pink and ecru become the canvas. They are sun-washed, never stark, and printed over with oversized monstera, hibiscus and daffodil.
