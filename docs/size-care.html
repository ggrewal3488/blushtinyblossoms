How to measure

  1. HeightStanding straight against a wall, without shoes, from the floor to the top of the head.
  2. ChestAround the fullest part of the chest, under the arms, with the tape level and comfortably loose.
  3. WaistAround the natural waist, where the sharara or pyjama will sit.
  4. HipAround the fullest part of the hips, feet together.
  5. LengthFrom the highest point of the shoulder, next to the neck, down to where the garment should end.

Fit tips

  • Between two sizes? Choose the larger one.
  • Buying a gift? Go one size up.
  • Shararas and lehngas sit at the waist and are cut with tiered volume, scaled up for the older sizes.
  • Still unsure? Send us height and chest with your child’s age and we will guide you before you pay.
