How to measure

  1. HeightStanding straight against a wall, without shoes, from the floor to the top of the head.
  2. ChestAround the fullest part of the chest, under the arms, with the tape level and comfortably loose.
  3. WaistAround the natural waist, where the sharara or pyjama will sit.

Send us these with your child’s age if you are between sizes, and we will guide you before you pay.

Good to know

Questions, answered

How do I order?

Choose a look, pick a size and add it to your enquiry bag, then send it to us on WhatsApp or Instagram. We confirm availability and everything else on chat before you pay.

Why does the price change with size?

Each look is priced in five size bands, from 3–4Y up to 12–13Y. Pick a size on any look to see its price.

Which sizes do you make?

Every look comes in ten sizes, from 3–4Y to 12–13Y.

My child is between sizes. What should I do?

Send us height and chest measurements along with their age. We will suggest the size that fits best.

How long does delivery take?

We confirm dispatch and delivery time on chat before you pay.

How do I care for the outfit?

Dry-clean only. Professional dry-cleaning preserves the print and the embroidery.
