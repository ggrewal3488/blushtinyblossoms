Care

Dry clean only. Professional dry cleaning keeps the print, gota and embroidery at their best.

  • Let it air out after wear before putting it away.
  • Store folded in a muslin or cotton bag, away from direct sunlight.
  • Keep perfume and deodorant away from the handwork.
